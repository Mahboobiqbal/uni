# Design System: University of Swat

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Swat Valley Editorial"**

The University of Swat presents itself as a dignified, modern public research university rooted in the Swat Valley — a landscape defined by Gandharan Buddhist heritage, alpine ecology, and a 1.2 MW solar park that made it the first 100% solar-powered public university in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The design system turns that institutional reality into a visual language: warm, spacious, editorial pages with a scholarly serif voice, restrained forest/crimson/gold accents, and photography-led storytelling.

The system is typography-first. Cormorant Garamond carries authority and heritage on display headlines, section titles, and pull quotes; Plus Jakarta Sans keeps complex data — programs, fees, merit lists, syllabi — legible and calm. The canvas is a warm alabaster (`#F6F5F0`) rather than pure white, which softens long-form reading and gives the deep forest green and crimson accents room to act as *accents*, never wallpaper. Motion is reserved for hover lifts, focused reveals, and meaningful transitions — never decorative looping.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm alabaster page canvas (never pure white) with deep charcoal ink.
- Scholarly serif display paired with a crisp geometric UI sans.
- Forest green = academic action; crimson = statutory authority (portal, deadlines, executive); gold = heritage (Roman numerals, architectural borders).
- 1360px reading grid with generous section rhythm (80px desktop).
- Cards are editorial surfaces: white, hairline stone borders, soft shadows — not colorful SaaS tiles.
- Real institutional photography (campus, leadership, departments) over stock clichés.

## Colors

A heritage palette rooted in the valley: deep forest greens (alpine flora), university crimson (executive authority), and muted gold (Gandharan architectural heritage), all on warm alabaster.

### Primary
- **Forest Obsidian** (#0C1912): high-prestige hero, video overlays, dark card containers. Used on the homepage hero and dark CTA bands.
- **Warm Alabaster** (#F6F5F0): primary page canvas across all non-hero sections.
- **Deep Forest Green** (#153B27): primary buttons, active tab indicators, section highlights, faculty accents.
- **University Crimson** (#7F1D1D): Student Portal button, deadline badges, executive alerts, split-header eyebrows.

### Secondary
- **Muted Architectural Gold** (#C8A96E / #B48A3E): top borders on hero cards, Roman numerals, heritage pins, lightbox focus ring.

### Neutral
- **Deep Charcoal** (#14291D): primary headings and high-emphasis labels.
- **Stone scale** (#FAFAF9 → #1C1917): body text, muted text, hairlines, surfaces, and border tones.

### Named Rules
**The Rarity Rule.** Forest green and crimson are chosen, not scattered. The primary accent appears on ≤10% of any given screen — usually one button, one active tab, or one eyebrow. Their rarity is the point.

**The No-Pure-White Rule.** Pages never rest on `#ffffff` as the canvas; white is reserved for card surfaces, and the surrounding canvas is always warm alabaster.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Cormorant Garamond (Georgia, serif fallback)
**Body Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (system sans fallback)
**Label/Mono Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (tabular numerals for data)

**Character:** A 16th-century calligraphic serif (Cormorant) meets a crisp, modern geometric sans (Jakarta). The pair delivers institutional gravity without feeling archaic, and clarity without feeling corporate.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, clamp(2.125rem→4.5rem), 1.08, -0.025em): hero and page-heading H1s only.
- **Headline** (700, clamp(1.75rem→3rem), 1.12, -0.02em): major section headers (VC discourse, bulletin, rankings).
- **Title** (600, clamp(1.25rem→1.5rem), 1.25, -0.01em): card headlines, faculty names, event titles.
- **Pull quote** (italic 400, 1.25–1.5rem, 1.4): Vice Chancellor discourse blockquote.
- **Body** (400, 0.9375rem, 1.65): reading text, syllabi, notices. Ceiling: 70ch line length.
- **Label/Eyebrow** (700, 0.6875rem, 0.1em tracked): section eyebrows, statutory tags, category labels.

### Named Rules
**The 70ch Rule.** Reading paragraphs never exceed 65–75 characters per line (`max-w-[70ch]`); full-width text blocks are banned.

**The Tight-Leading Rule.** Serif display >32px must use leading 1.08–1.15. Default 1.5 HTML leading is forbidden on display sizes.

**The No-Single-Italic Rule.** Headings never contain a single italic word; italics are reserved for full quotes (and latin species names in biology).

## Layout

A shared 1360px wide grid (`max-w-[1360px] mx-auto px-6 lg:px-12`) centers content across all pages. Section headers use a **split layout**: eyebrow + serif title on the left, right-aligned descriptive paragraph on the right (≤512px). Sections stack with vertical rhythm of 48px (mobile) → 64px (tablet) → 80px (desktop). A `PageHeader` component standardizes sub-page heroes with a back link, eyebrow, H1, and description.

Responsive: all grids collapse from 3–4 columns → 2 → 1. Navigation is a frosted sticky header (transparent over hero → frosted on scroll) with a 2-column mega-menu dropdown system and a mobile drawer.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is **flat-by-default with tonal layering**: depth comes from hairlines and surface tone, not heavy shadows. Cards rest on `shadow-xs` (1px hairline + 1px soft shadow). Hover raises cards with a forest-tinted shadow (`0 16px 36px -18px rgba(21,59,39,0.25)`) and a 2px lift. Dark sections (obsidian) use their own surface tone and never rely on shadows for separation.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Level 0 (rest):** `0 1px 2px rgba(28,25,23,0.04)` + 1px stone border — default card.
- **Level 1 (hover):** `0 16px 36px -18px rgba(21,59,39,0.25)` — interactive cards (faculty, programs, events).
- **Emphasis (dark band):** gradient from `#153B27` → `#14291D` with no shadow — CTA bands, leadership cards.

### Named Rules
**The Flat-By-Default Rule.** Surfaces are flat at rest. Shadows appear only as a response to hover, focus, or state.

## Shapes

Cards and content blocks use **12–24px radius**: buttons and inputs `12px` (rounded-xl), cards `16–24px` (rounded-2xl/3xl), chips and badges fully rounded. Hairlines (`1px rgba(231,229,228,0.9)`) define edges. The hero's three architectural cards carry a distinctive `2px gold top border` + Roman numeral markers — the signature shape moment.

**The Gold-Cap Rule.** The only place gold is allowed as a *border* is the hero card top-edge (`border-t-2 border-[#C8A96E]`); elsewhere gold reads as text/icon.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 12px radius (rounded-xl); 13–16px semibold Jakarta; 2px lift on hover + press-to-flat on active.
- **Primary:** forest `#153B27` bg, white text; hover `#0F2D1E`; used for academic CTAs (Explore Programs, Apply, Download).
- **Crimson:** `#7F1D1D` bg, white text; reserved for the Student Portal and statutory actions.
- **Secondary:** white/stone surface, stone border, charcoal text; hover stone-50.

### Chips / Badges
- Pill-shaped (rounded-full), stone-100 surface + stone-700 text, or emerald-tinted for active category pills (`bg-emerald-50/80 border-emerald-600/30` with forest text).
- Critical notices use solid crimson pill with white text.

### Cards / Containers
- White surface, hairline stone border, `shadow-xs`, 24px internal padding (p-6/8).
- Hover: forest-tinted shadow + −2px translate; optional image zoom within frame.
- Card categories: program, faculty, department, news, event, research, campus, stat, quick-link, announcement, deadline, location, gallery.

### Inputs / Fields
- Search bar: white field, stone border, focus-within swaps border to forest/50. 12px radius, 2.5 vertical padding.

### Navigation
- Sticky transparent overlay at top; on scroll → frosted `#0C1912/55` + `backdrop-blur-xl`, white/15 hairline.
- 6 mega-menu columns with icon + label + description entries; sliding active-tab pill on bulletin category rail.
- Mobile: full drawer with accordion submenus.

### Signature Component — PageHeader
Split sub-page hero: back link, crimson hairline eyebrow, serif H1 (up to 52px), right-aligned description. Background alabaster with faint gold/forest corner blobs (`blur-3xl`).

### Signature Component — ProfileCard (React Bits)
3D-tilt portrait card for the VC with glass frame, gold/maroon accents, and a footer where the name sits beside the crest in forest green.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use the split header pattern for every hub page (title left, muted right rail).
- **Do** use real institutional photography: campus, leadership, department labs, news events.
- **Do** keep reading blocks ≤70ch and body copy at stone-600 (`#57534E`) or darker.
- **Do** reserve gold for heritage accents; forest for actions; crimson for statutory, deadline, and portal moments.
- **Do** animate transform/opacity only; hover lifts ≤2px; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use pure white as a page canvas; warm alabaster is the only page background.
- **Don't** scatter accents; one forest/crimson moment per screen.
- **Don't** invent metrics, testimonials, or staff bios; every statistic on the site traces to HEC/GreenMetric/THE or the university's own published notices.
- **Don't** use gradient text, emoji icons, or SaaS-style pastel gradients.
- **Don't** stack identical three-column card grids on every page; alternate directories, timelines, feature stories, and grid layouts.
