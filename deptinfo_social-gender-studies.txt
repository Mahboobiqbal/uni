Department of Social and Gender Studies
Department of Social and Gender Studies
The Department of Social and Gender Studies encompasses multidisciplinary approach in social sciences that extends from orthodox sociological theories to heterodox sociological concepts. This multidisciplinary approach provides students a unique environment to be trained and educated with their natural potential recognized and unleashed. We do not focus only on conventional methods of learning rather facilitate students the way they are interested in. They are equipped with the skills and commitment to contribute to local and global development process. Their understanding of the social world and social problems is properly generated and regenerated in such a way that the difference between their actual and potential realization is reduced.
