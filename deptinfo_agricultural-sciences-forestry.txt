Department of Agricultural Sciences and Forestry
Department of Agricultural Sciences and Forestry
Introduction:
Department of Agricultural Sciences and Forestry in University of Swat was established in 2014 with the aim to focus on the sustainable management and promotion of Forests conservation for their biological diversity, ecological, recreational, scientific and educational values and quality water production functions for the greater good of the people of Pakistan and the world at large. This department will provide quality education with relevance by acquiring high qualified staff and state of the art equipment and facilities in the field of forestry.

WHY TO CHOSE FORESTRY?
The BS Forestry program at the Department of Agriculture Sciences & Forestry, University of Swat offers quality education, experienced faculty, modern laboratories, and extensive field-based learning in the ecologically rich forests of the Hindu Kush region. Students gain practical skills in forest management, biodiversity conservation, climate change, watershed management, wildlife, and environmental sustainability through research and hands-on training. With the increasing global demand for professionals in climate resilience, ecosystem restoration, and natural resource management, graduates enjoy diverse career opportunities in government, academia, research, NGOs, and international organizations while contributing to a more sustainable future for Pakistan.
BS Forestry Admission Requirement: Open Merit – HSSC with at least 45% marks (Pre-Medical / Pre-Engineering)
M.Phil. Forestry Admission Requirement: At least 2.5/4.0 CGPA in: Bachelors (16 years) in Forestry / Forestry & Range Management / Wildlife management / Zoology / Botany / Geology / Environmental Sciences)
